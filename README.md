# Miguel Ramos — AI Operations Portfolio


I operate AI bot systems for real businesses and am building toward AI Operations roles. This portfolio collects my case studies and projects.


## Case Study #0 — Online Shop Automation
A print-on-demand shop on Etsy and TikTok Shop (fulfilled by Printify), run by 7 AI bots organized by role — Manager, Accountant, Designer, Listing Creator, Researcher, Customer Service, and Pricing. The system creates 7 new listings per week and maintains existing listings on 2-3 hours per week of my time.
